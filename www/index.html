Generational Wealth

Live a whole financial life — from 18 to your legacy years. Make real-world money decisions, handle life's surprises, and watch how your choices grow (or drain) your family's wealth over decades.

About

Generational Wealth Roadmap is a free money game from WGRALGO / The Wealth Gap Resolution Algorithm™ Inc. Live a whole financial life, from 18 to your legacy years, and see how your choices grow (or drain) your family's wealth.

How it works

  • Pick who you are at 18.
  • Make 10 decisions across 5 life stages, including surprise life events. Investments grow, debts cost interest, and time passes between stages.
  • At the end, see your Family Wealth Score, your finances at 65, and how much is likely to reach the next generation.

A simplified game model for learning, not a financial prediction or financial advice.
