How It Works
  1. You walk through 12 life stages, from money mindset to legacy planning.
  2. Each stage gives a short scenario and 3 realistic choices.
  3. Every choice updates 7 metrics: Wealth Score, Emergency Fund, Credit Health, Debt Risk, Investment Growth, Family Stability, Legacy Impact.
  4. You see immediate feedback and a short money lesson.
  5. At the end you get a Family Wealth Story, rating, strongest and weakest category, and 3 personalized lessons.
