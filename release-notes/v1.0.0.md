# Generational Wealth Roadmap v1.0.0

First GitHub-ready release of the WGRALGO Generational Wealth Roadmap
Android app.

## Highlights

- Offline 12-stage life decision roadmap with 36 realistic choices
- Seven long-term metrics: Wealth Score, Emergency Fund, Credit Health,
  Debt Risk, Investment Growth, Family Stability, Legacy Impact
- Immediate feedback plus short money lessons on every choice
- Final Family Wealth Story summary, rating, strongest / weakest area,
  and three personalized lessons
- Ratings: Legacy Builder, Wealth Strategist, Foundation Builder,
  Still Building, Restart the Roadmap
- Black and gold WGRALGO design language
- Fully offline &mdash; no `INTERNET` permission, no ads, no analytics,
  no trackers

## Install

1. Download `GenerationalWealthRoadmap-v1.0.0.apk`.
2. Optionally verify the SHA-256 against
   `GenerationalWealthRoadmap-v1.0.0.apk.sha256`.
3. Allow installs from unknown sources on your Android device and open the
   APK.
