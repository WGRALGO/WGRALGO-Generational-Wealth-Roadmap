# Generational Wealth Roadmap v2.0.0

Works on phones and tablets, in portrait and landscape. Like earlier versions, it works offline, with no ads, analytics, trackers, or account.

## What's new
- **New game:** pick who you are at 18, then make 10 decisions across 5 life stages, including surprise life events. Investments grow, debts cost interest, and years pass between stages.
- **Family Wealth Score** out of 100, your finances at 65, how much is likely to reach the next generation, and a family protection checklist.
- **Looks like a real app:** black launch screen with the big logo, new launcher icon, solid app bar, and About / Privacy / Credits panels.
- **Back button works:** asks before leaving a roadmap, returns to the start from results, and asks before exiting.
- **Phones and tablets, portrait and landscape:** the app rotates freely, and the start screen fits landscape phones.
- The APK is now named `WGRALGO-GenerationalWealthRoadmap-v2.0.0.apk`, like every WGRALGO app.
- Still **100% offline**: no internet permission.

## Important: uninstall v1.0.0 first

This release is signed with a new key, so it can't install over v1.0.0. Uninstall the old app first. The app saves nothing, so nothing is lost.
